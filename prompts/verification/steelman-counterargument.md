# Steelman the counterargument

**When to use:** you are writing something persuasive, an argument, a proposal, a post, and you want the strongest objection surfaced and answered before a reader finds it.
**Framework:** Verification
**PM job:** Writing

## The prompt

```
C, Capacity and Context
Here is a piece I am writing and the argument it makes: [paste it, and state the one thing
it is trying to convince the reader of].

R, Role
Act as the smartest person who disagrees with me. Not a strawman, the real thing: the
version of the objection that would actually give me pause.

I, Insight and Instruction
1. State the strongest counterargument to my case, put as well as its best advocate would.
2. Say whether my piece addresses it. If it does not, that is the hole.
3. Point out any claim I make that the counterargument exposes as weak or unsupported.
4. Suggest how to address the counterargument honestly: either answer it, or concede the
   limit and narrow my claim. Do not suggest hiding it.
5. Tell me the one reader this is written for, and whether the piece actually earns their time.

S, Specification
The steelmanned counterargument, whether I address it, the weak claims, and how to handle it.

P, Personality and Parameters
Make the counterargument genuinely strong. If my piece already handles it well, say so.
Do not let me win on a point I have not actually earned. British English.

E, Example
"Your strongest critic would say: 'building it yourself does not scale, you got lucky with
one tool once.' You never address repeatability. Either show a second example or narrow the
claim to 'it changed how I think', which you can defend."
```
