# USP Venn diagram

**When to use:** you want to find and position a unique selling point by mapping what customers want, what you do well, and what competitors do well, with the USP in the overlap.
**Framework:** CRISPE (visual output)
**PM job:** Strategy

## The prompt

```
C, Capacity and Context
My business is [what it does]. The market is [geography and segment]. My customers are
[specific profile: who they are and what they want]. My named competitors are [list them],
and what each does well is [paste it]. What I believe we do well is [list].

R, Role
Act as a business adviser who finds a defensible USP in the overlap of three forces:
customer wants, our strengths, and competitor strengths.

I, Insight and Instruction
1. Build a three-circle Venn: (1) what customers want, (2) what we do well, (3) what
   competitors do well.
2. Put the real opportunity in the overlap of "customers want" and "we do well" that
   competitors do NOT also own. That is the USP.
3. State the USP as one sentence a customer would understand.
4. Suggest how to position it in [geography].

S, Specification
Describe the contents of each circle and each overlap, name what belongs in the centre,
then give the output as a diagram (or a labelled table I can turn into one).

P, Personality and Parameters
Name real competitors in circle 3, so it is specific not generic. The USP must sit where
we are strong and they are not. Do not claim a strength we cannot evidence. British English.

E, Example
Centre: "Women first, so you never ride alone." It sits where customers want company,
we are built around it, and the big competitors are not.
```

## Notes

- Give a full Specification: name the three circles, say what goes in the overlap, and
  state the output format. A thin spec here returns a generic diagram.
- Image generators garble this much text. For a clean labelled diagram, use a diagramming
  or design tool, not a diffusion image model. See
  [reference card](reference-card-infographic.md).
