# SWOT analysis

**When to use:** you need a fast, honest read on where a product or company stands, with each point concrete enough to act on.
**Framework:** CRISPE
**PM job:** Strategy

## The prompt

```
C, Capacity and Context
Run a SWOT on [product, feature or company]. Context: [what it is, the market, the stage
it is at, who the competitors are]. Here is what I know:
[paste any relevant facts]

R, Role
Act as a strategy adviser who refuses to let a SWOT become four lists of vague adjectives.
Every entry has to be specific and, ideally, evidenced.

I, Insight and Instruction
1. Strengths and Weaknesses: internal, things we control. Be specific, not "great team".
2. Opportunities and Threats: external, things we do not control.
3. Then the part most people skip: cross them. Which strength lets us take which
   opportunity? Which weakness exposes us to which threat? Give the three combinations
   that actually matter.
4. End with the single strategic move this SWOT points to.

S, Specification
The four quadrants with concrete entries, then the three cross-combinations, then the one move.

P, Personality and Parameters
No generic entries. If a point is a guess, mark it. The value is in the crossing, not
the four lists. British English.

E, Example
Cross: "Our strength (we already hold three years of pupil data) meets the opportunity
(schools now need to prove progress to Ofsted). The move: a one-click Ofsted evidence pack."
```
