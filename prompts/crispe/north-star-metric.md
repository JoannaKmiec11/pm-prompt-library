# North star metric

**When to use:** you need one customer-centric metric the whole company can rally behind, plus the input metrics that actually drive it.
**Framework:** CRISPE
**PM job:** Growth

## The prompt

```
C, Capacity and Context
Help me define a north star metric for [product]. Our business model is [how we make
money]. The value customers get is [the core value]. Our vision is [vision]. Metrics we
track today: [paste them].

R, Role
Act as a metrics strategist. You know a north star is a single, customer-centric leading
indicator of value received. It is not revenue, not a basket of metrics, and not an OKR.

I, Insight and Instruction
1. Classify the business game: Attention, Transaction or Productivity.
2. Propose a single north star metric and check it against all seven criteria: easy to
   understand, customer-centric, reflects sustainable value, aligns with the vision,
   quantitative, actionable, and a leading indicator of success.
3. Define 3 to 5 input metrics that most directly drive the north star and are easier to
   move in the short term.
4. Name the trap: the way this metric could go up while customers get less value, and
   the guardrail that catches it.

S, Specification
The game, the proposed metric with the seven-criteria check, the input metrics, and the
gaming trap plus guardrail.

P, Personality and Parameters
Not revenue, not a vanity count, not multiple metrics. If the obvious candidate fails a
criterion, say which one and why. British English.

E, Example
"Weekly active teachers who viewed a pupil's progress is customer-centric and leading.
Guardrail: if it rises while weekly logins per teacher fall, we are nagging, not helping."
```
