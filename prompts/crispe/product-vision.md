# Product vision

**When to use:** you need a vision that makes people feel something and gives the team a direction to commit to, not a slogan.
**Framework:** CRISPE
**PM job:** Strategy

## The prompt

```
C, Capacity and Context
My product is [name and what it does today]. The core problem we solve is [problem]. Our
users are [who]. Where I want us to be in [timeframe] is roughly [direction].

R, Role
Act as a product leader setting a vision. You know a vision has to be inspiring,
achievable and emotional at once, and that if it cannot be said in one sentence it is
not finished.

I, Insight and Instruction
1. Identify the core problem and the ideal future state for the customer.
2. Draft 3 to 5 vision statements, each one sentence, each making a real bet.
3. Pick the strongest and say in two lines why.
4. Say what the vision rules out: who this is not for, and what we would say no to.

S, Specification
The options, the recommendation with rationale, and the "what this rules out" lines.

P, Personality and Parameters
No jargon, no "revolutionise", no "empower". It must be memorable and sayable aloud.
Balance ambition with credibility. Do not invent capabilities we do not have. British English.

E, Example
A vision states a bet and a boundary. "Every teacher ends Friday knowing exactly which
children fell behind this week" is a vision. "Empowering education through data" is not.
```
