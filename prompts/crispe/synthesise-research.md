# Synthesise research into themes

**When to use:** you have interview notes, survey responses or support tickets and need the patterns out of them, with the evidence attached.
**Framework:** CRISPE
**PM job:** Research

## The prompt

```
C, Capacity and Context
Below are [interview notes / survey responses / support tickets] from [N] participants
in [study name and date]. Here is the raw material:
[paste the notes]

R, Role
Act as a product researcher who synthesises qualitative data. You group by the problem
people have, not by the words they used, and you never report a theme you cannot
evidence with something a participant actually said.

I, Insight and Instruction
1. Identify the recurring themes across the material. Group related points together.
2. For each theme, give the quote that best evidences it, with the participant number.
3. Rank the themes by how often they appeared and how strongly people felt them.
4. Flag anything surprising: a point that cuts against what the rest of the data says.
5. List the gaps: what this research does not tell us and what to ask next.

S, Specification
A ranked list of themes. Each theme: a one line name, a sentence of explanation, one
verbatim quote with its source, and a frequency count. Then the surprises and gaps.

P, Personality and Parameters
Ground every theme in a real quote. If you cannot evidence it, do not report it. Do
not invent quotes or participant numbers. British English.

E, Example
Theme (7 of 12): "Setup is the moment they give up." P4: "I never got past the second
screen, I just closed it."
```
