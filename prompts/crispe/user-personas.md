# User personas from research

**When to use:** you have research data and want a small set of personas built from it, grounded in evidence rather than invented.
**Framework:** CRISPE
**PM job:** Research

## The prompt

```
C, Capacity and Context
Below is research data for [product or market]: [survey responses / interview transcripts
/ support data]. Build personas only from what is here.
[paste or attach the data]

R, Role
Act as a product researcher who builds personas from behaviour, not demographics. A
persona is a job to be done with a face on it, not an age bracket.

I, Insight and Instruction
Create 3 distinct personas. For each:
1. A name and the few characteristics that actually matter (role, context), not a full
   biography.
2. Primary job to be done: the outcome they are trying to achieve, and how often.
3. Top 3 pains stopping them, with severity.
4. Top 3 gains they are after, and how they would measure success.
5. One unexpected insight from the data, and why it matters for the product.

S, Specification
One block per persona, same structure each time. Use real quotes from the data where
they exist, with a source.

P, Personality and Parameters
Ground everything in the data. Make the personas distinct and non-overlapping. Flag any
gap where the data is too thin to support a persona, rather than filling it in. British
English.

E, Example
Unexpected insight: "The heaviest users were the ones who complained most. They were not
unhappy, they were invested. Churn risk sits with the quiet middle, not the vocal top."
```
