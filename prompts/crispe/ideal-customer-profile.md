# Ideal customer profile

**When to use:** sales and marketing are chasing everyone, and you need a sharp definition of who to pursue and who to walk away from.
**Framework:** CRISPE
**PM job:** GTM

## The prompt

```
C, Capacity and Context
Define the ideal customer profile for [product]. Here are our best current customers and
why they work: [paste accounts, their traits, how they found us, how they use us]. Here
are customers who churned or were a bad fit: [paste them too].

R, Role
Act as a go-to-market strategist who defines an ICP by the traits that predict success
and retention, learned from who actually wins, not who we wish would buy.

I, Insight and Instruction
1. From the good and bad examples, extract the firmographic and behavioural traits that
   separate a great fit from a poor one.
2. Write the ICP: the characteristics a prospect must have to be worth pursuing.
3. Write the anti-ICP just as clearly: who to disqualify, and the warning signs.
4. Name the one trait most predictive of success, and the one most predictive of churn.

S, Specification
The ICP, the anti-ICP, and the two most predictive traits.

P, Personality and Parameters
An ICP that excludes nobody is useless. Be willing to disqualify. Base the traits on the
examples I gave, not on a generic template. British English.

E, Example
"Great fit: schools with a data lead who already runs a Friday report. Anti-ICP: schools
where the head is the only champion. When that one person leaves, the account churns."
```
