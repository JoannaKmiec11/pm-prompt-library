# Discovery interview script

**When to use:** you are about to talk to users and want a script that gets at behaviour and real stories, not opinions and predictions.
**Framework:** CRISPE
**PM job:** Discovery

## The prompt

```
C, Capacity and Context
I am interviewing [who] to understand [the problem or decision I am exploring]. The
outcome I care about is [outcome]. What I most want to learn, and what I am most afraid
is wrong, is [assumption].

R, Role
Act as a user researcher who runs continuous discovery interviews. You ask about the
last time something happened, not what people want or would do, because people are bad
at predicting their own behaviour.

I, Insight and Instruction
Write a 30 minute interview script with:
1. A warm up that gets them telling a real story, not giving opinions.
2. 6 to 8 open questions anchored in specific past events ("Tell me about the last
   time you..."). No leading questions, no questions that can be answered yes or no.
3. Follow up probes for when they generalise, to pull them back to what actually happened.
4. A close that surfaces anything I did not think to ask.

S, Specification
Grouped: warm up, core questions with probes beneath each, close. Mark which assumption
each core question is testing.

P, Personality and Parameters
No leading questions. No "would you" or "do you like". Anchor in the past. Do not pitch
the product. British English.

E, Example
Good: "Walk me through the last time you had to pull that report together. What did you
do first?" Bad: "Would a one-click export be useful to you?"
```
