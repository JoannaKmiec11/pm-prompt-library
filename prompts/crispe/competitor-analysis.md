# Competitor analysis

**When to use:** you need the competitive landscape mapped and, more importantly, where the gap is that you could own.
**Framework:** CRISPE
**PM job:** Research

## The prompt

```
C, Capacity and Context
My product is [name and one line on what it does], in the [market / segment]. Our users
are [who] hiring it for [job]. Here is anything I already know about the field:
[paste notes, or ask it to research]

R, Role
Act as a competitive intelligence analyst. You care less about a feature grid and more
about where the market is not being served, because that is where differentiation lives.

I, Insight and Instruction
1. Define the market and the job customers are hiring these products for.
2. Identify 5 direct competitors. For each: positioning, who they serve, pricing model,
   two real strengths, two real weaknesses or gaps.
3. Map the differentiation opportunities: unmet needs, segments nobody serves well, jobs
   solved badly across the whole set.
4. Recommend one positioning for my product and the single differentiator to lead on.

S, Specification
A short market definition, a table of the 5 competitors, then the opportunities and the
one recommendation.

P, Personality and Parameters
Distinguish direct competitors from adjacent alternatives. Where you are inferring rather
than certain (pricing, market share), say so. Do not state guesses as facts. British English.

E, Example
Gap: "All five lead on reporting for leadership. None of them build anything for the
teaching assistant who actually enters the data. That is the underserved user."
```
