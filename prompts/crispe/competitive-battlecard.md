# Competitive battlecard

**When to use:** sales keeps meeting a specific competitor and needs a one-page card to handle it honestly and win.
**Framework:** CRISPE
**PM job:** GTM

## The prompt

```
C, Capacity and Context
Build a battlecard against [competitor] for [our product]. Here is what I know about
them and about how deals against them tend to go:
[paste their positioning, pricing, strengths, and where we win or lose]

R, Role
Act as a product marketer who writes battlecards a rep can use in a live call. You are
honest about where the competitor is genuinely better, because a card that pretends
otherwise gets the rep caught out.

I, Insight and Instruction
1. One-line summary of how they position themselves.
2. Where we win: the two or three points where we are genuinely stronger, with the proof.
3. Where they win: where they are genuinely better, and how to handle it without lying.
4. Landmines: questions our rep can ask that expose their real weaknesses.
5. Objection handling: the three objections they will seed about us, and the honest response.

S, Specification
A one-page card: their position, where we win, where they win, landmines, objection
handling. Short, scannable, usable live.

P, Personality and Parameters
Honest about their strengths. No trash-talk, no claims we cannot back. If I have not told
you how a deal usually goes, ask rather than inventing a win story. British English.

E, Example
Landmine question: "Ask them how long an export takes for a whole trust. If it is still
batch-overnight, that is the gap we close in real time."
```
