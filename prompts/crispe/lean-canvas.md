# Lean canvas

**When to use:** you want a new idea pinned to one page, with the riskiest parts exposed rather than the easy parts polished.
**Framework:** CRISPE
**PM job:** Strategy

## The prompt

```
C, Capacity and Context
Help me fill a Lean Canvas for [idea]. What I know so far: [the problem, who has it, and
any solution thinking]. Be honest about what I have not worked out yet.

R, Role
Act as a startup adviser who uses the Lean Canvas to find the weakest box, not to make
the idea look complete.

I, Insight and Instruction
Work through the nine boxes:
1. Problem (top 3) and existing alternatives.
2. Customer segments, and the early adopters within them.
3. Unique value proposition, and the high-level concept.
4. Solution (top 3 features).
5. Channels. 6. Revenue streams. 7. Cost structure.
8. Key metrics. 9. Unfair advantage.
Then name the two boxes that are weakest or most assumption-heavy, and the cheapest way
to test each.

S, Specification
The nine boxes filled concisely, then the two weakest boxes and their tests.

P, Personality and Parameters
Do not pad a box to look complete. An honest "we do not know yet, here is how we would
find out" is worth more than a confident guess. British English.

E, Example
Weakest box: "Unfair advantage is blank, and 'we care more' is not one. The only real
candidate is the three years of data schools cannot export elsewhere. Test whether that
actually locks them in."
```
