# Outcome roadmap

**When to use:** you need a Now / Next / Later roadmap organised around outcomes and problems, not a feature-dated Gantt chart that will be wrong by Friday.
**Framework:** CRISPE
**PM job:** Execution

## The prompt

```
C, Capacity and Context
Build a roadmap for [product]. The outcomes we are pursuing are [outcomes or OKRs]. The
opportunities and problems we know about are [paste them]. Our constraints: [team size,
timeframe, hard dependencies].

R, Role
Act as a product manager who builds roadmaps around outcomes and problems, so the plan
survives new information instead of committing to features and dates nobody can keep.

I, Insight and Instruction
1. Organise into Now, Next and Later.
2. Each item is a problem or outcome to pursue, with the candidate solutions underneath,
   not a committed feature.
3. For Now items, state the outcome we expect and how we will know it worked.
4. Make the dependencies and the biggest risks explicit.
5. Say what moved to Later and why, so the de-prioritisation is a visible decision.

S, Specification
Three columns, Now / Next / Later, items phrased as outcomes. Then dependencies, risks
and the "moved to Later" notes.

P, Personality and Parameters
No hard dates unless a real external deadline forces one. Problems and outcomes, not a
feature list. Do not imply certainty about Later. British English.

E, Example
Now: "Reduce the number of schools that never finish setup (currently 40% stall). Betting
on a guided first-run, but the import step may be the real blocker, testing that first."
```
