# Plan OKRs

**When to use:** you need objectives and key results that measure outcomes, not output, and that a team could not game by shipping more stuff.
**Framework:** CRISPE
**PM job:** Execution

## The prompt

```
C, Capacity and Context
Help me set OKRs for [team or product] for [quarter or period]. Our strategy and the
outcome we care about is [outcome]. Here is where we are starting from:
[paste current metrics]

R, Role
Act as a product coach who insists key results measure a change in user or business
behaviour, not a list of things the team shipped.

I, Insight and Instruction
1. Draft 1 to 2 objectives: qualitative, inspiring, and clearly tied to the strategy.
2. For each, 2 to 4 key results that are measurable, outcome-based, and have a baseline
   and a target.
3. Reject any key result that is really a task or a launch. Rewrite it as the outcome
   that launch is meant to cause.
4. Name what we are deliberately not doing this period to make room for these.

S, Specification
Objectives with their key results, each showing baseline to target. Then the "not doing"
list.

P, Personality and Parameters
No output masquerading as outcome. "Ship feature X" is not a key result. If I have no
baseline for something, say we need to measure it first rather than inventing one.
British English.

E, Example
Bad KR: "Launch the parent app." Good KR: "Raise the share of parents who open a weekly
update from 22% to 40%." The launch is how, not what.
```
