# Leadership update

**When to use:** weekly or monthly, to give leadership progress, risks and the one decision you need, without burying it in detail.
**Framework:** CRISPE
**PM job:** Writing

## The prompt

```
C, Capacity and Context
Draft this period's update for leadership on [product or team]. Here is what happened:
[progress, metrics, blockers, decisions pending]. The audience is [who, and what they
care about]. The outcome we are working towards is [outcome].

R, Role
Act as a product manager writing for busy executives. You lead with the decision you need
and the risk they should know, because that is what they are reading for.

I, Insight and Instruction
1. Open with a one-line status: on track, at risk, or off track, against the outcome.
2. Progress: the two or three things that actually moved the outcome. Not a task list.
3. Risks: what could derail it, and what you are doing about each.
4. The ask: the single decision or unblock you need from them, stated plainly.
5. Keep detail in an appendix they can skip.

S, Specification
Status line, progress, risks, the one ask, optional appendix. Short enough to read in
two minutes.

P, Personality and Parameters
Lead with the ask and the risk, not the activity. No hedging. If something is off track,
say so plainly rather than softening it. Tie everything back to the outcome. British English.

E, Example
Status: "At risk. Signups are up but activation is flat, which is the metric that matters.
The ask: approval to pause new features for one sprint and fix onboarding."
```
