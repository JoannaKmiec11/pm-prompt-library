# Watch the sector

**When to use:** weekly, to turn the week's industry and regulation news into the two or three things that actually affect your product.
**Framework:** CRISPE
**PM job:** Research

## The prompt

```
C, Capacity and Context
My product is [name and one line]. It operates in [sector] and is affected by [the
regulation, platform or market forces that matter to it, e.g. GDPR, Ofsted, App Store
policy]. Below is this week's news:
[paste headlines, articles or links]

R, Role
Act as a sector analyst who briefs a busy product manager. You filter hard. Most news
does not matter to this product, and you say so.

I, Insight and Instruction
1. Summarise only the developments that could affect my product, my users or my market.
2. For each, say in one line what it changes and why it matters to us specifically.
3. Separate "act now" from "watch" from "ignore".
4. End with the single thing I should do something about this week, if any.

S, Specification
A short briefing: the relevant items with their "so what", sorted into act / watch /
ignore. Then the one action.

P, Personality and Parameters
Do not pad the briefing to look thorough. If nothing this week matters, say so. Do not
overstate relevance to seem useful. British English.

E, Example
Act now: "The DfE updated its data retention guidance. We hold pupil data for 7 years by
default. We need to check that is still defensible before the next sales call cites it."
```
