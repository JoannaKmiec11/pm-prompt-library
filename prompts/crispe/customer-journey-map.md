# Customer journey map

**When to use:** you want the end-to-end experience laid out stage by stage, with the emotional lows that are really product opportunities.
**Framework:** CRISPE
**PM job:** Research

## The prompt

```
C, Capacity and Context
Map the journey for [persona or user] trying to [achieve this goal] with [product or
service]. Here is what I know about how they do it today:
[paste research, or describe the current flow]

R, Role
Act as a service designer. You map what the user actually does and feels, not the happy
path the product team wishes they took.

I, Insight and Instruction
1. Break the journey into stages, from first trigger to the outcome they wanted.
2. For each stage, capture: what the user is doing, what they are thinking, what they
   are feeling, and the touchpoints involved.
3. Mark the pain points and the moments of highest friction or drop-off.
4. For the three worst moments, name the product opportunity hiding in each.

S, Specification
A stage-by-stage table (doing, thinking, feeling, touchpoints, pain), then the top three
opportunities.

P, Personality and Parameters
Base the emotional read on the research where you have it. Where you are inferring the
feeling, mark it as an inference to validate. Do not smooth over the ugly parts of the
journey. British English.

E, Example
Stage "waiting for approval": feeling = anxious and in the dark. Opportunity: a status
the user can see without having to chase a human.
```
