# Stakeholder map

**When to use:** a decision or launch touches a lot of people and you need to know who to involve, when, and how.
**Framework:** CRISPE
**PM job:** Execution

## The prompt

```
C, Capacity and Context
I am [launching / deciding / changing] [describe it]. The people and teams involved are
[list them with their roles]. Here is what I know about where each one stands:
[paste what you know]

R, Role
Act as a product manager who maps stakeholders to avoid the two classic failures: blind-
siding someone who should have been consulted, and drowning in approvals from people who
only needed to be informed.

I, Insight and Instruction
1. Place each stakeholder on influence (high / low) against interest (high / low).
2. For each, recommend how to engage: manage closely, keep satisfied, keep informed, or
   monitor.
3. Name each one's likely position and what they most care about.
4. Flag the one relationship most likely to derail this if neglected, and how to handle it.
5. Suggest the sequence: who to talk to first, and why order matters here.

S, Specification
A 2x2 placement, a per-stakeholder engagement plan, and the sequence.

P, Personality and Parameters
Be honest about politics without being cynical. Where I have not told you someone's
position, say it needs finding out rather than assuming it. British English.

E, Example
"The Head of Data has low interest but high influence. Keep satisfied, not closely managed.
Blindside them in a steering meeting and they can stop this with one objection about GDPR."
```
